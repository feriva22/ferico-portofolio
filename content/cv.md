### About me

I am a **Professional IT DevOps and Cloud Engineer** working at enterprise scale, with hands-on experience across cloud-native platform engineering, DevSecOps automation, and data centre operations.

My expertise spans **multi-cloud** environments on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and Alibaba Cloud, container platforms on **Kubernetes** and **Docker**, infrastructure as code with **Terraform** and **Ansible**, and the CI/CD pipelines and security guardrails that let product teams release safely. I run observability end to end — metrics, logs, and distributed traces collected through **OpenTelemetry** into **Prometheus** and **Grafana**.

I began my career building applications end to end as a fullstack engineer, which is why I approach platforms from the perspective of the engineers who deploy on them. I care about infrastructure that is understandable and dependable: described in code, tested in a pipeline, observable in production, and automated so that toil does not accumulate.

My current focus is an **OpAMP control panel for fleet management of OpenTelemetry Collectors**, enabling automatic observability agent setup across environments rather than manual per-host configuration.
