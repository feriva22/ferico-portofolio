## The content is used for the description of the project
---

Designing and operating workloads across **AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and Alibaba Cloud** — deliberately multi-cloud, with each service chosen for cost, compliance, and workload fit rather than provider loyalty.

Every environment is provisioned with **Terraform** and configured with **Ansible**, so infrastructure is versioned, reviewable, and reproducible. Kubernetes clusters are standardised across providers so that application teams get a consistent deployment target regardless of which cloud the workload lands on.

The goal is straightforward: no snowflake environments, no undocumented manual steps, and the ability to rebuild any environment from code.
