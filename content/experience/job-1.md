## Fullstack Engineer — PT. Fokus Jasa Mitra (Petrokimia Gresik Group)

Built and owned internal business applications end to end for a company within the Petrokimia Gresik Group, covering everything from database design and API development through to deployment and production support. Working in a small engineering team meant close collaboration with operations, finance, and field teams to turn manual processes into reliable digital workflows.

## Key contributions

- **End-to-end application delivery** — designed, built, and shipped web applications using modern backend and frontend stacks, including data modelling, REST APIs, authentication, and responsive interfaces.
- **Production deployment and operations** — containerised services and managed releases across Linux environments, replacing ad-hoc manual deployments with repeatable, documented procedures.
- **CI/CD foundations** — set up automated build and test pipelines so every change was validated before reaching production, reducing release effort and regression issues.
- **Infrastructure and observability** — configured reverse proxies, load balancing, and log/metric collection to give the team real visibility into application health and performance.
- **Security and data integrity** — implemented role-based access control, input validation, and scheduled database backups to protect business-critical data.
- **Cross-functional delivery** — worked directly with non-technical stakeholders to scope requirements, estimate effort, and prioritise the work with the most operational value.

This role formed the foundation for my move into platform engineering: the more applications I delivered, the clearer it became that reliable infrastructure, automation, and observability are what let teams ship safely at speed.
